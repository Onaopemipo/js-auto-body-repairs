## Legal review

The legal pages are operational website templates.

Before launch, the business should confirm:

- the legal business name
- the correct contact email
- the actual hosting provider
- the actual email provider
- whether Cloudflare Turnstile is enabled
- whether analytics is enabled
- information-retention periods
- any insurer or third-party disclosure practices
- any privacy obligations that specifically apply to the business

Professional Australian legal review is recommended.
